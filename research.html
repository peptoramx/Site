RESEARCH BRIEFS.

Original PEPTORA notes for compounds people most often compare. Each brief separates the mechanism, the question being studied, the evidence setting and the limits of inference.

CLINICAL · METABOLIC

RETATRUTIDE

Mechanism. A triple agonist studied across GLP‑1, GIP and glucagon receptors.

Research question. How multi-incretin signalling changes weight and metabolic endpoints in defined clinical populations.

Compare. Tirzepatide, Cagrilintide and Mazdutide are related metabolic conversations, not interchangeable molecules.

Boundary. A phase‑2 obesity trial is clinical evidence in a defined population; it is not a general biohacking outcome.

MECHANISTIC · MITOCHONDRIAL

MOTS‑c

Mechanism. A mitochondrial-derived peptide studied in metabolic and exercise-related signalling.

Research question. How circulating and tissue-level MOTS‑c relates to acute exercise and metabolic state.

Compare. NAD+ and SS‑31 concern cellular-energy conversations through different biology.

Boundary. Human observations of endogenous MOTS‑c do not demonstrate a performance result from a product.

PRECLINICAL · NEUROSCIENCE

SEMAX

Mechanism. An ACTH(4–10) analogue investigated in neurotrophic and BDNF/TrkB-related research.

Research question. Whether specific neural signalling changes occur in experimental systems.

Compare. Selank belongs to stress/GABA conversations; it is not the same cognitive profile.

Boundary. Rodent BDNF findings do not establish a personal focus or neuroplasticity outcome.

REGULATORY · ENDOCRINE

TESAMORELIN

Mechanism. A GHRH analogue considered in endocrine and visceral-adiposity discussions.

Research question. Its evidence must be read within the labelled population and indication.

Compare. CJC no DAC and Ipamorelin are different GH-axis research references.

Boundary. Regulatory context does not support generic body-composition claims.

PRECLINICAL · TISSUE

BPC‑157 / GLOW

Mechanism. Tissue-response, actin-dynamics and dermal-signalling conversations span distinct components.

Research question. BPC‑157, TB‑500, GHK‑Cu and KPV must be read separately before a blend is discussed.

Compare. GLOW is BPC‑157 + TB‑500 + GHK‑Cu; KLOW adds KPV.

Boundary. Component literature cannot be added together as clinical evidence for a blend.

MECHANISTIC · CELLULAR

NAD+

Mechanism. A central cellular redox cofactor, not a peptide.

Research question. Which redox, metabolic and mitochondrial measures change in a given study context.

Compare. MOTS‑c and SS‑31 intersect with cellular-energy conversations but do not share one mechanism.

Boundary. Central biological importance is not proof of a longevity outcome from any formulation.
