## Pricing and synchronization

The most recently changed retail price for a matching presentation wins. Initial price recency was reconstructed from the inventory's Git commit history. Future lot edits update the derived `data/store-sync-state.json` alongside the public offers feed in the **same atomic commit**. Stock-only changes do not replace price recency. This fixes the former acquisition-date / array-order rule that could select an older duplicate lot's price.

English price per vial = rounded `(MXN / 18.2) + 5` USD. Spanish price remains the inventory's MXN retail price. Cart and WhatsApp use the same displayed unit-price rule. Spanish WhatsApp +52 662 462 1816; English WhatsApp +1 731 666 5112. Out-of-stock products remain orderable by inquiry, with the stated 5–7 day shipping estimate after purchase confirmation. In-stock quantities are never displayed or loaded from the public feed.

The store refreshes on load, focus and every minute; GitHub CDN propagation can add delay. Opening WhatsApp is not a sale, reservation or payment. Confirm sales in the existing inventory system to update availability. Direct GitHub edits outside that app bypass its atomic update hook; normal inventory UI operations are the supported workflow.

If feed verification fails, the store falls back to price/availability confirmation, not a fabricated price or stale available count.
